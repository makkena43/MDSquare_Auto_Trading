# Interpretation, execution and data-quality evaluation

Version 0.8.0. This is the evaluation/readiness contract, not a backtest result or a collector implementation.

## Three separate records

1. **Interpretation:** cutoff, available source revisions, model/skill/config/context hashes, raw request/response, swing ancestry, parent regime, setup sequence, opposition, stop/target rationale and consistency disposition. Review expert agreement and disagreements without showing later candles. An interpretable plan can lose; a rejected idea can win.
2. **Execution:** decision request, response, validation and arm timestamps; trigger before/after arming; expiry, rejects, fills/ambiguities, protective events and exits. Link to the immutable interpretation ID. Missed latency is not a historical fill or proof a setup was wrong.
3. **Outcomes:** original fixed plan and declared units/costs, realized episode results, adverse/favorable excursions and session/block uncertainty. Do not use outcome excursions as earlier initial risk or replace the original judgment with a later audit.

Measure collector finality, queue delay, provider response time and validator/arming delay in shadow mode; record distributions and failures by provider. Select a latency/expiry convention before a frozen outcome test. A made-up one-minute delay is a hypothetical sensitivity, not measured cloud performance. Compare fixed decisions under declared timing sensitivities without manufacturing retroactive fills. Compare different identification versions as distinct logged experiments.

## Source admission and repair

Require exact instrument, calendar, timezone/interval labeling, native source, provenance/revision, received/available timestamps or explicit estimates, expected gaps/duplicates, raw-to-canonical aggregation and interval quality flags. Completeness and hashes do not prove genuine prices. Repeated flat bars, conflicting duplicates and unnatural closing sequences are suspect, not automatically fake.

The supplied September/October 2026 community data has repeated identical zero-range candles over 15:15–15:25 in all ten sampled sessions. Cause remains unverified. Mark those intervals SUSPECT; retain raw rows and the audit. Do not forward-fill or delete them to improve results. Reconcile the same instrument/timestamps against an independently obtained primary/broker source, preserve discrepancies and transformations, and release only after source semantics and values are explained. Access/backfill integration is still required; no repair has been performed by this skill.

Quality disposition is scoped: unusable active setup evidence blocks new affected candidates; suspect later data does not retroactively change an earlier valid cutoff. Existing protection remains independently monitored. A replay with missing credible exit observations cannot invent reliable P&L; show unavailable/ambiguous outcomes or a separately declared data-aware research policy.

## Untouched evaluation

Keep every inspected date and candidate audit in development, including all ten existing sessions. Freeze skill/model/config, method conventions, policy, data manifests, baseline and reporting criteria before selecting chronological validation/holdout sessions. Do not label a future calendar period “untouched” without confirming available data and inspection status.

Partition by sessions/blocks, include quiet, trending, ranging and failed-transition examples, and preserve preceding warm-up context without importing future facts. Declare when a inspected holdout becomes development; use new data for further claims. Public prices may be known to models; no claim of pretraining blindness. Prospective paper observation is separate evidence.

Report context/ancestry/obstacle disagreements, abstention reasons, opportunity coverage, accepted/missed/expired plans, provider errors, true latency, episode returns/drawdown, cost sensitivities and sample dependence. No daily trade quota, automated winner selection, fabricated win probability or promotion based solely on months elapsed. Operational and economic thresholds remain owner-configured.
