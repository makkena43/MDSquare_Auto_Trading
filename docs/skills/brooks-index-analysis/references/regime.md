# Market context, location and change

## Four layers, not one direction label

Record higher-timeframe background, current intraday regime, local leg and signal sequence separately. Daily bullish location can coexist with an intraday bear trend. A local bull leg inside a range is not necessarily a bull trend. A daily reversal shape does not determine a five-minute entry.

Use the analysis timeframe supplied by the application, normally 5m, with bounded 15m/60m and daily/weekly context. Additional timeframes clarify a genuine question; do not hunt across timeframes until one endorses the desired trade. Data retention is not the prompt size.

## Regime evidence

| Regime / subtype | Supporting observations | Contradictions to examine | Working behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bull trend, tight/small pullback | Upward displacement, sustained strong closes, limited overlap/opposing depth, defensible rising structure | Material downside displacement, lost structure, repeated return into former range | Favor qualified long continuation; initial shorts are possible corrections |
| Bear trend, tight/small pullback | Corresponding downside evidence | Material upside displacement, lost structure, repeated return into former range | Favor qualified short continuation |
| Broad directional channel | Directional progress with deeper overlap and two-sided legs | Failure of progress or increasing repeated rejection at shared boundaries | Selective continuation near structure; weaker urgency than tight trend; not automatic opposite-edge trading |
| Wide trading range | Earlier-established boundaries, repeated rejection/return, two-sided overlap, sufficient usable room | Sustained acceptance beyond an edge; boundaries unsupported before cutoff | Long near lower boundary, short near upper; target a defensible internal reference |
| Tight trading range | Dense two-sided overlap, weak progress, repeated reversals/tails, little usable travel relative to structural risk | Genuine escape with acceptance outside; a short pause still embedded in a strong directional leg | No new candidates inside; observe escape |
| Transition | Structural challenge and opposing strength with follow-through/test evidence, prior thesis weakening | First weak opposing bar, minor line break only, rejection of the proposed escape | Maintain alternatives; trade only a fully supported enabled thesis |
| Unknown | Inadequate or conflicting structure | More data may resolve it | WAIT or INSUFFICIENT_DATA |

These observations support interpretation; they are not a fitted weighted score. A moving-average cross, high/low pair or big candle cannot determine regime alone. Numerical tolerances, width criteria and confirmation counts must come from versioned policy; do not manufacture them as Brooks rules. Missing optional numeric regime thresholds does not prevent qualitative classification when evidence is clear.

## Persistent swing interpretation

Use [swing context](swing-context.md) to retain initiating move, defended structure, phase and evidence across updates. A local corrective leg does not by itself change the parent swing. The daily background cannot mechanically override an intraday swing. Require cited structural change for a transition; ambiguity remains explicit.

## Tight versus wide

Assess boundaries, candle scale, overlap, coherent swings, structural invalidation and available distance after confirmation. Width in index points alone is not enough. A wide parent range can contain tight local congestion; apply the no-trade rule at the proposed entry structure.

Distinguish a compact directional flag from an independent two-sided range using the preceding impulse, corrective behavior and current progress. Do not call all overlapping flags forbidden, or rename two-sided congestion a flag to bypass the rule. If that distinction remains unresolved, WAIT.

No universal percentage such as bottom 20%/top 20% is supplied. Identify supported edge zones and explain proximity using actual levels. Exact day low/high is unavailable in advance. An H2 in the range middle is not improved by a clean count. At a valid lower edge, an H2 may support a range-failure thesis; it does not create a second continuation trade.

## Inertia without rigidity

The default is continuity of observed market behavior, not perpetual direction. In a trend, distinguish a minor countertrend attempt from evidence of an emerging opposite trend. In a range, distinguish a strong internal leg/edge excursion from sustained outside acceptance.

For any proposed transition record:

- Previous regime and the evidence that supported it.
- Structure challenged, its prior identity and the bars demonstrating the challenge.
- Opposing displacement/close quality, follow-through or test, and what remains unresolved.
- Evidence that would confirm the new thesis and evidence that would return interpretation to the old one.

A meaningful breakout can begin new context before a complete textbook reversal exists. Do not demand a second retest for every kind of breakout unless that setup's frozen definition requires one. Conversely, the enabled breakout-pullback IDs do require an actual pullback/test; a pure breakout cannot be renamed to satisfy them.

Higher-high/lower-low alone does not establish acceptance. Repeated outside closes, low overlap, controlled tests or continued progress may support it; exact requirements depend on the supplied setup policy. Once credible acceptance is observed, stop blindly fading the old edge. Reversals and range rotations are different: selling near a wide-range upper edge is not necessarily attempting a major bear trend.

Brooks's [market-inertia discussion](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/price-action/what-is-price-action-6-aspects/) describes frequent failures of trend reversals and range breakouts. Treat the 80% formulation as qualitative guidance; neither a per-trade win rate nor an empirical result for these indices.

## Causal level map

Use a small relevant set of prior session/week extremes and closes, current observed opening/edges, confirmed swings, identified range boundaries and breakout/test areas. Cite creation/confirmation bar IDs or timestamps. Never redraw a historical range using later success.

Identify the first meaningful obstacle and classify it as checkpoint, major opposition or unresolved before choosing a target. A supported measured-swing policy can target beyond a checkpoint; major/unresolved opposition cannot be ignored. A measured move is a scenario, not guaranteed travel. Explain whether the proposed signal is at support/resistance, an actual test, extended beyond structure or inside congestion. Strong bar appearance cannot repair poor location.

## Challenging the preferred thesis

State the strongest contrary fact and the most plausible alternative, rather than a generic caution. Example: 'bull continuation remains plausible, but repeated closes below the breakout boundary favor a return to the range.' Explain the next observable discriminator. Do not supply arbitrary percentages or use model confidence to rank trades.

## Phase and evolving session profile

Use [Trends/Trading Ranges phase guidance](trends-and-ranges.md#2-spike-tight-channel-broad-channel-and-range) to distinguish initiating spikes, tight directional channels, mature channels, broad stairs and independent ranges. Fresh-entry urgency/room can weaken before a holding thesis fails. Record session-profile hypotheses (trend from open, spike/channel, trending ranges, possible resumption or developing reversal) at the current cutoff; these are not mutually exclusive final-day labels. A long consolidation after a morning trend permits observation of either escape, not a forecast of late resumption. Broad-channel tests can overlap earlier breakout boundaries without automatic reversal. No copied US-clock or range-fraction threshold is supplied.
