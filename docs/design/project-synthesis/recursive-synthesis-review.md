## Pass 3: plan and policy feasibility

Check compatibility before outcome testing. For an upward breakout of a base with lower L, upper U and height H=U−L, a full-base stop L−b and entry U+e have risk H+e+b. A one-height target U+H offers reward H−e. With positive displacement/buffer/friction, it cannot meet a 2R initial-risk gate. This mathematical issue is independent of subsequent prices.

Invented illustration: L=100, U=110, entry=110.05, stop=99.95, target=120. Risk is 10.10 and reward 9.95 before costs. The interpretation may be reasonable, but this particular stop/target/ratio combination is incompatible. Do not shorten protection, extend the objective without evidence or report only the winning examples. A different qualified entry or independently supported objective/policy is a separate registered alternative.

Likewise, a higher-timeframe target with a minor-pause stop, a range-middle H2 labeled continuation, or future adverse excursion used as entry-time risk is a consistency failure. Holding a valid swing through normal corrections and exiting an invalidated premise are compatible requirements; neither mandates waiting for every stop or closing on every contrary signal.
