# Resolving context, structure and trade-management ambiguity

Skill guidance v0.9.0. Apply these requirements to the existing swing map, opposition ledger and plan-consistency contract. They refine judgment; numerical conventions remain separately registered. No application or validated edge is supplied.

## Preserve the four layers

Record daily/weekly background, current intraday regime, parent swing/range rotation and local signal separately. Day type is provisional at this cutoff. A local bull leg in a parent range does not create a bull trend day; a daily bear background does not forbid a supported intraday bull swing. Resolve the entry from the applicable parent structure and opposition, rather than averaging these layers into a score.

Distinguish **observed** prices/crossings, **interpreted** structure and **configured** confirmation rules. A model can interpret a defended lower high; it cannot invent its prices, silently choose a new pivot threshold or treat a later confirmed turn as earlier knowledge.

## Full correction versus nested pause

1. Identify the initiating leg and its observed endpoint at this cutoff. Inspect the full subsequent opposing activity, including inside bars, tails, overlap and failed resumptions. Do not start the correction only at the first lower-low/higher-high crossing or the latest opposite-colored bar.
2. Retain the earliest relevant opposing activity and all its extreme prices while corrective behavior remains unresolved. Reference segments and attempt evidence. A nested pause receives its own parent link; it cannot replace the parent correction.
3. An attempted resumption remains an attempt until the configured directional-progress/confirmation rule is met. Crossing a nearby signal trigger alone does not establish a completed parent correction.
4. Late breakout acceptance revises the interpretation of already-observed structure. It does not shift the historical correction start to the acceptance candle. Reconstruct the initiative's actual extreme and subsequent correction from the available prefix.
5. A distinct new correction requires an evidenced end to the previous one and new opposing activity after directional progress. Name the criterion and bars; do not reset merely to allow another entry.
6. When two defensible ancestry interpretations produce materially different stops or counts, preserve both and WAIT for the affected entry. If earlier bars are missing, use INSUFFICIENT_DATA. Do not select the interpretation with the better ratio.

Initial protection for a new pullback entry can reference the full correction once the supplied resumption evidence supports it. This does not grant permission to tighten an existing position before its declared trailing rule. Neither the nearest pause nor the entire day's extreme is automatically the correct stop.

## Flag, range and genuine context change

| Evidence at the cutoff | Working interpretation | Entry consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supported impulse, controlled opposition, defended structure and credible resumption | Corrective flag within the swing | Assess an enabled continuation route; overlap alone is not a veto |
| Sustained two-sided acceptance, shared-edge rejection and lost directional progress | Independent range or range developing inside the parent | Tight range: no entry. Wide range: assess supported outer-edge rotations |
| Strong internal range leg reaches its defended outer edge | Range leg or possible transition | Do not assume a directional swing through that edge |
| Meaningful boundary challenged, configured outside acceptance and credible opposing-effort failure | Supported new initiative | Assess breakout family without automatically requiring an extra pullback |
| Mixed evidence or unresolved prominence/acceptance convention | Unresolved context | WAIT; identify what would distinguish the alternatives |

No fixed overlap ratio, bar count or retracement percentage becomes Brooks doctrine. Record the governing registered convention where one is needed; an unresolved convention is a requirements gap, not missing price data.

## Checkpoint versus barrier

Evaluate the origin and scope of each relevant level, the approach, rejection strength, repeated defense, actual outside acceptance and the parent regime. A previous high can be a checkpoint in a supported trend and major resistance in a persistent range. Neither a universal veto nor a universal “trend will pass it” rule is acceptable.

| Assessment | Required explanation | Target treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CHECKPOINT | Evidence supports passage; specify the observable rejection that would defeat it | A permitted beyond-level target may remain a scenario/objective |
| MAJOR_OPPOSITION | Defended parent boundary or meaningful opposing evidence defeats the proposed passage thesis | Constrain to a justified nearer objective or SKIP this plan |
| UNRESOLVED | Passage is plausible but acceptance/structure is not established | WAIT for the discriminator, or assess an independently supported inward plan |

Separate `passage_plausible` from `outside_acceptance_observed`. The breakout family may act under its registered initiating confirmation policy; it need not wait for every later checkpoint to break. Conversely, an internal range entry cannot borrow that exception. Classifications require evidence, not forecast certainty or an invented probability.

## Match entry, protection and objective

Choose intent and eligible setup before calculating the ratio. Identify what event defeats that particular thesis, then choose the stop reference and configured buffer. Choose the move the context supports, then calculate economics. Recompute at prospective/eligible entry without changing the thesis to rescue a late trade.

| Entry thesis | Protection reference | Objective evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First/second pullback in supported swing | Full relevant correction or a separately evidenced defended swing | Declared prior-leg/equal-leg scenario or justified first opposition |
| Initiating accepted breakout | Relevant base/defended initiating structure | Causal range/initiating-leg projection; meaningful remaining travel |
| Breakout test and resumption | Full actual test, with parent structure considered | Defended boundary plus supported projection/opposition passage |
| Wide-range failed breakout | Full failed excursion/edge structure | Inward rotation; midpoint/other registered internal objective |
| Enabled opening reversal | Full failed opening initiative/test | Evidenced new swing or justified first opposition; no gap-only target |

There is no universal minimum point distance or mandatory 2R. Record bar-size/remaining-session diagnostics without inventing cutoffs. Probability of objective-before-stop remains unverified unless compatible calibration is supplied. A wider stop can improve tolerance and still make the plan unattractive; skip rather than stretch the target. Never change original filled geometry using later favorable candles.

## Hold versus exit: precedence and evidence

1. Read authoritative inventory, fills, protective events and pending actions first. Already completed/pending exits do not receive duplicate close recommendations. Missing state means REVIEW_REQUIRED for that position.
2. Known stop/target/session-policy events take precedence. The application determines event ordering/fills; the model cannot declare them from an ambiguous candle.
3. If no hard event applies, compare the original defended structure and passage thesis with the new evidence. Normal small opposing bars or a checkpoint touch alone do not invalidate a supported swing.
4. Sustained opposing acceptance, structural defeat, or evidenced loss of the original objective's premise can justify CLOSE under the declared exit policy before the stop. Cite the defeated condition, not merely the existence of an opposite setup.
5. A newly forming range weakens continuation urgency but does not impose an automatic exit. Review remaining room, opposing effort and the registered timeout/exit policy. HOLD while unresolved if the policy permits it; missing policy/critical evidence requires REVIEW_REQUIRED rather than an invented timed exit.
6. Keep original plan, current thesis and proposed action separate. No stop widening, automatic reversal, partial fraction or add is introduced. Planned additions remain WATCH_ONLY without explicit bounded policy.

## Required concise explanation

State the controlling parent context, full structure reference, strongest contradiction, disposition and next discriminator. A CANDIDATE includes its consistency evidence and permitted numeric plan; WAIT/SKIP contain no armable plan. Avoid rehearsing every principle on each candle. Show the one unresolved distinction that actually changes the decision.
