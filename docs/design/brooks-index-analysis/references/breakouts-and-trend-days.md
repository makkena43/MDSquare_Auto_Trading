# Breakouts and trend-day opportunities

Focused requirements synthesis. Read when deciding whether a breakout or trend continuation deserves a swing objective. This refines the existing unified skill; it adds no strategy ID, indicator, execution style or automatic enablement. Shorts mirror the long examples, without assuming equal empirical results.

## The controlling question

Is price escaping an established boundary, continuing a supported directional swing, or accelerating late into an obstacle? A strong-looking bar alone cannot answer this. Read what preceded it, what it actually broke, and the response available by this candle. Judge the opportunity now; do not wait to label the eventual completed day.

Keep the normal flow: supplied position review → context → location → enabled sequence → one structural stop and justified objective → decision. Record only the distinction that changes the decision. The descriptions below are working interpretations, not more required schema fields or separate engines.

## Four useful working contexts

| Behavior visible so far | Participation emphasis | Evidence against that interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh directional escape / strong spike | Eligible initial breakout or first controlled pullback; supported swing objective | Failure to progress, material opposing follow-through, acceptance back in the old balance |
| Small-pullback trend / steep tight channel | With-trend participation; first-entry or H2/L2 as their actual sequence allows | Corrections increasingly large relative to this trend's earlier corrections, lost defended structure, sustained two-sided acceptance |
| Spike followed by a less urgent channel | Buy supported bull corrections / sell supported bear corrections; assess room before each new entry | Repeated rejection at new extremes, deep overlapping correction, mature-channel acceleration into opposition |
| Directional move between trading ranges | Assess the actual escape/test, with more caution about distant swing targets | Quick return to the earlier range, a new accepted balance, stronger opposing progress |

These are refinements of existing context, not permanent whole-day labels. An opening trend can become balance and later resume. A daily trend-shaped close cannot be used to justify an entry earlier that day. Tight directional channels differ from tight horizontal congestion: the former can support continuation; the latter blocks new entries until qualified escape. Broad, increasingly flat channels require range-like location judgment; a succession of higher highs does not make every new high a swing buy.

## Existing routes, different reasons to enter

| Opportunity | Existing route | What must be established now | What invalidates or postpones this route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First escape from known balance or a meaningful opposing structure | `breakout_continuation_long/short` | Boundary known before the break, displacement and registered acceptance, prospective trigger, defensible base protection and room | Merely internal prior-bar break, unresolved rejection, trigger already missed, stop/objective conflict |
| First controlled correction after a supported initiative | `bull_first_pullback` / `bear_first_pullback` | Actual first corrective attempt, intact premise, completed resumption evidence | Requiring H2 despite a valid enabled first-entry route; or calling an untested missed spike a pullback |
| Renewed correction followed by second resumption attempt | `bull_high2` / `bear_low2` | Actual attempt sequence, parent swing support, whole relevant correction and remaining target room | Count-only signals, uninterrupted opposing channel, late entry at major opposition |
| An actual test of the established break | `breakout_pullback_long/short` | Prior break, real corrective test, defended structure and completed resumption | Invented retest, developing test, sustained opposing acceptance |
| Late escape from balance after an earlier directional move | Existing breakout/test or actual second-entry route | Earlier initiative retained as background, current boundary and present acceptance, usable time and geometry | Assuming the morning direction must win, trading inside tight balance, entering after the registered cutoff |

Breakouts beyond a meaningful extreme in an existing strong trend are a distinct interpretation of `breakout_continuation_long/short`, not necessarily a fresh initiating spike. They require an explicitly registered operational variant and switches; earlier initial-escape-only policies/results are not silently expanded. Prefer a genuine earlier pullback opportunity when still available, but do not create a historical fill. A new extreme with sustained progress differs from a brief penetration repeatedly rejected. A late breakout above a mature bull channel, or below a mature bear channel, needs particular exhaustion scrutiny. It is neither automatically a fresh leg nor automatically a countertrend entry.

H1/L1 can be appropriate in very strong context. H2/L2 is useful when the actual correction supplies a second attempt; it is not a universal waiting rule. A weak-looking signal can support a strong parent trend under the declared qualification policy. This does not waive a configured strong-signal-bar filter or permit discretion to evade it. Distinguish source interpretation, policy exclusion and missing evidence.

## Breakout quality without a scorecard

Use the strongest relevant evidence: displacement through meaningful structure; bodies, closes, tails and overlap; progress after the break; depth and duration of the correction; the reaction at a new extreme; and whether a material opposing barrier remains. Sustained progress through several relevant levels supports strength more than a marginal break immediately rejected. A weak first reversal that fails can support continuation; successful opposing follow-through can defeat it.

Evidence arrives in stages. At the initial breakout close, later follow-through, the eventual pullback and profitable travel for a hypothetical scalper are unknown. At the next candle, use only what has now occurred. More confirmation can improve interpretation while making entry too late or leaving too little reward. Report that tradeoff, without retroactive admission. A valid pending plan retains its original trigger, expiry and identity as evidence arrives.

Five-minute OHLC cannot reveal how long an unfinished bar stayed near its high, the order of its internal excursions, or trader positioning. One-minute inputs can provide limited sub-bar sequence, but still do not reveal every tick; use them only if that input and policy are supplied. A microgap is an observable price relationship, not proof of participant motives or a guaranteed measuring gap. Volume is outside this project's chosen scope, even where source material discusses it. No numeric evidence score, universal two-close confirmation, or imported 40/60/80% forecast is added.

## Stops and measured objectives must describe the same trade

Select the thesis and projection anchors before checking reward/risk. Protect the structure that this entry depends on:

- Initial escape: relevant base/initiating structure under the registered variant. A tiny pause cannot automatically justify replacing that protection.
- New pullback or test entry: complete relevant correction/test, including an earlier nested extreme. It does not invariably require a stop at the origin of the entire preceding trend.
- Existing strong-trend extreme entry: defended corrective swing appropriate to that declared continuation variant; no unsupported one-bar stop to make the ratio look attractive.

Use one committed initial objective, from the existing economics reference:

| Thesis | Natural projection to assess | Causal limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Escape from established range | Range-height extension beyond its boundary | Bounds must exist before the escape; do not enlarge them using later bars |
| Strong completed spike | Spike extension from its endpoint | A developing spike has no final endpoint; do not use the eventual high/low |
| Resumption after a confirmed correction | Leg equality from the correction endpoint | Endpoint confirmation and chosen impulse origin must be available now |
| Plausible measuring gap | Existing declared gap projection method | Do not label every gap as a measured midpoint; exhaustion and unresolved gaps remain counter-evidence |

Default to the supported primary method rather than calculate every possible projection and select the most attractive. Preserve non-binding alternatives separately only if they matter. Two projections reaching similar prices are not independent probability estimates. A prior extreme can be a checkpoint in a supported swing; a major opposing barrier can make the full projection unsuitable. If its role remains unresolved, WAIT on that swing commitment. Reaching a projection can produce a pause, continuation or reversal; it does not authorize a fade.

Illustrative arithmetic, not an NSE trade: a range from 100 to 110 gives an upside range-height objective of 120. Entry 112 with full-base stop 99 risks 13 for reward 8 before costs. This does not satisfy a policy requiring 2R. Entry 109 after an independently qualified test, stop 104 and the same objective would instead risk 5 for reward 11. The latter cannot be manufactured retrospectively: it needs an actual valid test and future trigger under policy. Widening the objective to 138 or tightening the initial stop without structural support is not a remedy. A real breakout can be untradeable under the selected economic policy; wait for another eligible opportunity or preserve the miss.

For a known compatible two-outcome model, break-even success probability is `(risk + expected round-trip cost)/(reward + risk)`. It is a threshold, not a forecast. Early premise exits create additional payoff outcomes; evaluate their realized distribution separately rather than infer profitability from target-hit percentage alone. Contextual confidence and Brooks teaching frequencies do not establish an index-specific positive expectancy.

## Holding the trend without ignoring contrary evidence

For a supplied open swing, normal opposing bars, a small first correction or checkpoint touch do not automatically close it. Keep original protection/objective and review the premise. Compare the current correction with prior corrections in this trend, not a universal point threshold. Tight-channel trend strength can survive visually dramatic opposite bars if opposition gains little ground and fails to follow through.

Repeated failure to progress at new extremes, meaningful lost structure or sustained opposing acceptance warrants the supplied premise-exit policy. A large late trend bar may be exhaustion and deserves review; size alone is insufficient for an exit, reversal, stop move or target change. Do not convert an unachieved fixed swing objective into a scalp afterward. No automatic breakeven, trailing, partial profit, averaging down or reversal is introduced.

If price returns toward the channel origin, treat that known area as a possible test/magnet. Do not promise it will be reached or assume the entire initial spike is undone. A stop event ends its trade; the parent context may survive. A fixed target ends its trade; the market may continue. A later qualified setup needs distinct structure and registered reentry, not recycled signal identity.

## Opening and late-session adaptation

Use the prior session's late structure as well as daily background when supplied. A gap can continue, reverse or enter balance; it does not determine the day or guarantee gap fill. Cash-index, futures and official-close versus last-intraday-price gap conventions must be explicit. Early opposing initiatives can matter later even after an intervening swing changes direction.

Morning impulse → balance → accepted late resumption is a useful possibility, not a prediction or guaranteed same-direction breakout. While balance is tight, keep assessing without new entries. Assess a credible escape in either direction under the enabled route. Second-leg equality is conditional on current evidence and available anchors, not guaranteed daily range doubling. US source hours, bar counts, tick distances and occurrence rates remain source examples, not NSE rules. A sound interpretation can still be excluded by entry/holding cutoff or insufficient time; preserve that exclusion without relabeling it a bad setup.
