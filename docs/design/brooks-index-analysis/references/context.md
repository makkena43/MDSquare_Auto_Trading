# Data readiness and processor continuity

## History and cutoff

Application storage target is rolling 30 calendar days intraday plus at least 12 months daily, with derived weekly/monthly context. This is not an instruction to reject ordinary chart interpretation without a year of data. Require the history relevant to the claimed structure and disclose narrower conclusions.

Use instrument, source timestamps, interval/timezone semantics, finality/revisions, analysis cutoff, recent completed 5m structure, bounded 15/60m context and daily/weekly location. Retrieve older slices only for a specific unresolved question through available tools. Do not claim a collector/database exists when only a chart is supplied.

Supply the previous proposed/accepted swing thesis and revision, its evidence cutoff, phase, initiating/defended anchors, target mode/projection/checkpoints and relevant entry-route/episode state. Revalidate hypotheses against current bars; absent prior thesis means reconstruct causally, not infer missing exposure.

Useful supplied facts: completed prior session/week/month levels; current observed open/high/low; completed configured opening range; confirmed swings; causal EMA/ATR; overlap/body/tails; enabled catalog and current episode/plan state. Optional event/VIX/futures-volume facts need instrument/source/as-of provenance and are not mandatory pattern filters. Spot volume is unavailable, not zero traded volume.

Session boundaries follow the exchange calendar. Developing higher-timeframe bars are provisional. Historical data recovered/corrected later cannot become earlier knowledge. Final-day range/regime labels, future highs/lows and unpublished pivots are excluded. Fully visible future chart outcomes prevent blind evaluation even if the annotation asks for an earlier cutoff.

## Underlying-only scope

Analyze directional context and plans in index price units. Option quotes, contract discovery, monetary sizing and broker execution are deferred and not prerequisites. Do not imply that a valid underlying candidate is an actual fill or establishes profitability in any instrument. Submittable plans still need precise prices, identity and policy; qualitative review does not.

The application must supply authoritative open-trade state on every update: original thesis/stop/target anchors, actual fills/size, remaining exposure, amendments, pending recommendations, add-on policy, risk budget and state revision. This enables the same skill to identify new ideas and review existing trades. The skill cannot infer that missing inventory means flat, maintain durable positions by itself, or provide continuous stop protection between calls. Confirmed fill/exit events, not recommendations, change trade state.

## Handover, when relevant

Read supplied context/hash, config/skill versions, source/target processor, assignment generation, bar watermarks, accepted/pending/consumed episodes and expiries. If execution/account facts are supplied, preserve them; narrative cannot change them. Fetch deltas through available tools before claiming current state.

Without valid application identity/assignment, provide research analysis only. Do not submit a prior processor's result under the new generation, reissue an accepted episode, extend expiry or reset state. Prior regime/session summaries remain hypotheses to recheck against current evidence. No broker or protective-order action belongs to this skill.

## Structured continuity and quality (v0.8)

Supply the accepted swing-map revision, complete relevant correction segments, parent-range levels, opposition ledger, pending proposed amendments and interval quality/revision records with the context snapshot. Include deltas since that watermark. Handover preserves unresolved ancestry and obstacles rather than replacing them with a confident summary. Code rejects updates against stale base revisions/generations and archives them for inspection; it never silently overwrites accepted history.

Suspect source intervals narrow usable evidence. Use [evaluation and quality](evaluation-and-data-quality.md) for reconciliation and unavailable fill/exit handling. The skill must not call padding repair complete merely because raw timestamps are present.
