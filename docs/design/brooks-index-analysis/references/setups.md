# Setup qualification and switches

Categories are configuration buckets, not official Brooks taxonomy or independent edges. A setup name is never permission to trade. Apply the regime/location gate to every family, including breakouts. Numerical filters and operational parameter choices are research hypotheses supplied by versioned config, not proven Brooks rules.

## Swing research coverage profile

The v0.4 research profile covers twelve directional IDs. This supersedes the six-family coverage used in the frozen v0.3 replay, not its results or settings. Coverage is not automatic enablement: each route still needs supplied switches, complete operational signal/trigger/stop/expiry rules and applicable paper policy. Without config report research interpretations only; no application-ready decision or live permission follows.

| Category | Coverage IDs |
| --- | --- |
| breakout | breakout_continuation_long/short, breakout_pullback_long/short |
| bullish_momentum | bull_first_pullback |
| bearish_momentum | bear_first_pullback |

Category off overrides child on; direction/mode/definition/context eligibility also apply. Remaining catalog families are observations until separately configured and evaluated. Do not relabel a disabled first entry as H2, or a pure breakout as a pullback, to evade switches.

## Initiating breakout continuation

IDs: breakout_continuation_long / breakout_continuation_short. Require a causally known meaningful boundary, directional displacement beyond it and supplied acceptance/follow-through evidence. Identify what actually broke, the strongest rejection evidence, whether the entry is still prospective, structural invalidation and objective anchors. A prior-bar extreme alone is not necessarily a meaningful initiating boundary. This family does not require a pullback; breakout-pullback does. Do not demand a textbook reversal where strong opposing breakout establishes a supported new initiative, but do not equate one isolated spike with acceptance. A still-internal range leg, terminal/climactic extension, repeated return inside or unusable geometry defeats entry. Confirmation can make an entry late; missing/late triggers are not historical fills.

## First controlled pullback / H1-L1 participation

IDs: bull_first_pullback / bear_first_pullback. Require a supported initiating directional move and its first qualified corrective attempt, defended impulse structure, controlled opposition and completed resumption signal with a prospective configured trigger. Cite corrective origin and actual attempt evidence; one color change alone is insufficient. Do not force a second attempt before considering a configured first-entry route in strong context. Distinguish shallow directional flag from independent two-sided congestion using the swing reference. A deep correction that destroys the initiating premise, an already-triggered entry or inadequate room defeats the idea. If a first attempt triggers and renewed correction develops, a later H2/L2 can be a different sequence under the existing episode/reentry policy, never a retroactive rename.

## Qualified opening reversal

IDs: opening_reversal_bull / opening_reversal_bear. Require an actual opening directional attempt at a known contextual level, evidence that it failed, meaningful opposing displacement and the supplied confirmation/test policy. Cite the failed structure and opposing bars. A gap alone, assumed gap fill or first weak opposite candle is insufficient. Establish a new-direction swing only as current evidence supports it; daily location does not predetermine the day. Pure initiating reversal versus subsequent breakout/pullback are distinct entry stages. If the supplied opening policy requires a test, wait for it; do not impose one universally or use US session conventions. Stop references the actual failure structure; targets use the justified new swing or first major opposition. No prediction of the eventual whole-day regime.

## Common qualification

For a plausible setup ask: What behavior is continuing/changing? Where is it? Which bars establish the sequence? Is the signal complete, developing or already triggered? What is the strongest contrary evidence? Where are structural invalidation and the first opposing obstacle? Is the proposed entry still prospective and within supplied policy?

No candidate inside a tight range. Wide-range rotation must originate near an appropriate established edge with inward evidence and room; avoid interior congestion and blind edge fading. Prior daily alignment is context, not an automatic veto or endorsement. Record sequence ambiguity rather than force a label.

## H2 / L2 continuation

IDs: bull_high2 / bear_low2. Default continuation interpretation below; the explicitly registered wide-range variant is described at the end of this reference. Context: a defensible corresponding directional trend/leg and correction with continuation structure still plausible. Mild-category placement does not exclude strong trends.

Identify the corrective origin, first attempted resumption, renewed correction and second attempt. Reference individual bars and whether each attempt actually crossed its trigger. For a prospective signal the next configured crossing is not yet a fill. Equality/tick tolerance and nested sequence rules come from policy; unresolved counting means WAIT. Two green/red candles alone are not a second entry. Repeated higher-high/lower-low crossings inside an uninterrupted opposing channel may remain part of one corrective leg. Cite the intervening interruption/renewed correction; distinguish parent and nested sequences. See [corrective attempts](trends-and-ranges.md#4-pullback-hierarchy-and-actual-attempts).

Long thesis: corrective selling fails again and bullish behavior resumes; short mirrors it. Structural invalidation concerns the relevant correction, not automatically a one-bar stop. Use visible opposing levels for target room.

Reject treating H2 as a bull-continuation buy inside a strong bear trend without credible contextual change; corresponding rule for L2 in a strong bull trend. In a wide range the sequence may be supporting evidence for the appropriate edge-failure thesis instead. Reject interior congestion, lost continuation structure, extended late entry and insufficient room.

## Breakout pullback

IDs: breakout_pullback_long / breakout_pullback_short. Identify a boundary known before the break, a completed move beyond it, follow-through or defensible outside acceptance, an actual test/pullback and a completed resumption signal.

The thesis is that the challenged boundary no longer contains price and the test supports continued movement. The invalidation references the actual test/structure; target references opposing levels or a declared projection. Pure breakout, developing test and resumed breakout are different stages.

A test can cross back through the broken boundary without destroying continuation, particularly in broad channels; cite defended structure, opposing strength and renewed acceptance. Do not require every test to remain entirely outside. Reject one isolated spike, repeated return/acceptance inside, absence of the required test, chasing or insufficient room at eligible entry. A tight-range breakout is observable; no candidate until credible escape/context and this family's actual test/resumption are established. WAIT for missing confirmation, not an invented retest. Accepted outside movement changes range interpretation; stop blind fading.

## Wide-range failed breakout

IDs: range_failed_breakout_long / range_failed_breakout_short. Require a wide range established before the excursion, correct outer edge, failure to sustain outside, completed inward return and qualified signal. Long after downside failure; short after upside failure.

Thesis: the range continues, not a major opposite trend. Initial target is a defensible internal reference, not an assumed far-edge swing. Invalidation references the failed excursion/current structural thesis. Evaluate remaining distance after confirmation.

Reject tight-range/interior noise, invented boundaries, a still-strong accepted breakout, middle-of-range entries or a signal already too close to its target. A new high/low alone is not a failure. H2/L2 or double-test tags can support this episode without creating more trades.

## Additional families: contextual observation catalog

Families not in the twelve-ID coverage profile remain observation-only by default. The first-pullback and opening-reversal rows summarize the qualification sections above; their coverage does not imply automatic enablement. These are interpretation guides, not complete armable definitions. For all directional pairs apply the corresponding relationships; do not assume identical empirical performance.

| Family / IDs | Context and sequence that support observation | What defeats the interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening-range breakout: opening_range_breakout_long/short | Configured opening range complete, meaningful edge challenged, outside acceptance/confirmation; retest requirement must be declared | Incomplete range, one spike, repeated return, breakout against unresolved opposing structure |
| Strong first pullback: bull_first_pullback / bear_first_pullback | Established strong impulse, first qualified correction, controlled opposing activity and resumption evidence | Buying/selling the original missed impulse; deep two-sided correction changing context; invalidated impulse structure |
| Microchannel pullback: bull_microchannel_pullback / bear_microchannel_pullback | Clear directional microchannel, first break/pullback, evidence that countertrend effort remains limited | First opposing bar treated as a major reversal, arbitrary microchannel origin, too much late extension or acceptance against the trend |
| Double-test flags: bull_double_bottom_flag / bear_double_top_flag | Directional context, corrective second test holding relevant structure, resumption signal | Same visual shape at a terminal trend extreme or range middle; tests identified using future bars |
| Wedge pullbacks: bull_wedge_pullback / bear_wedge_pullback | Corrective pushes against a still-plausible trend, suitable level and resumption evidence | Three pushes counted without context, structural trend failure, unresolved congestion |
| Range double tests: range_double_bottom / range_double_top | Wide established range, second test near lower/upper edge, inward evidence and room | Tight range, interior tests, accepted outside break, automatic assumption of major reversal |
| Major trend reversal: major_reversal_bull/bear | Prior trend, meaningful opposing strength/structural break, test of old trend and new-direction evidence | First minor line break, one opposite candle, strong prior trend intact; do not label range rotation as major reversal |
| Wedge reversal: wedge_reversal_bull/bear | Prior directional extension, exhaustion/three-push interpretation near relevant structure, opposing evidence | Small corrective wedge supporting continuation, pushes chosen retrospectively, no opposition beyond a signal shape |
| Opening reversal: opening_reversal_bull/bear | Opening directional attempt fails at a known contextual level, new-direction strength and confirmation | Assuming every gap must close or importing US session timing; no established failure |
| Final-flag reversal: final_flag_reversal_bull/bear | Mature trend, meaningful opposing location/weakness, continuation flag attempt fails with reversal evidence | Naming a flag 'final' before evidence, ordinary early/mid-trend flag, strong continuation acceptance |

Magnets, measured moves and channels supply context/targets; they are not automatic additional entries. A large signal bar near exhaustion differs from an initiating breakout, even when both look strong.

## One episode and primary classification

Use supplied episode identity/state when available. Do not fabricate durable IDs from narrative. If no application episode is supplied, describe the overlap qualitatively and do not claim deduplication was enforced.

An application's supplied classification precedence governs. Without one, for research choose the thesis's defining mechanism: range-edge failure for range rotation; established boundary/test for breakout-pullback; otherwise second-entry continuation when supported. This is a disclosed annotation convention, not automatic order priority. For initiating/first-pullback/opening-reversal routes retain their defining mechanism; do not force them into the older three families. Preserve secondary tags and note uncertain overlap.

A plan already triggered/consumed/expired cannot become a fresh plan by renaming the pattern. Missing re-entry policy means describe any renewed opportunity without arming re-entry. Targets and expiry cannot silently extend. Recheck location, context and invalidation rather than recycle the previous signal.

Terminology reference: [Brooks pattern overview](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/price-action/10-best-price-action-trading-patterns/). This catalog establishes no measured index frequency or win probability.

## Existing strong-trend breakout variant

The breakout-continuation pair can cover a meaningful established trend extreme under an explicitly registered operational variant, as distinct from initial escape. Existing initial-escape-only policies/results are unchanged. Require supported parent strength, actual corrective/defended structure, meaningful extreme, prospective confirmation/trigger and room to the justified objective. Repeated rejection at extremes or late acceleration beyond a mature channel is counter-evidence. A prior-bar crossing alone does not qualify. No extra setup ID or automatic enablement is created. See [breakouts and trend days](breakouts-and-trend-days.md).

## Wide-range H2/L2 variant

An explicitly registered range-rotation variant of `bull_high2` / `bear_low2` can assess real inward second-entry sequences at the lower/upper edge of an established wide range without a failed excursion. This does not silently enable the variant or change old continuation-only trials. Require actual attempt evidence, correct edge, inward confirmation, structural stop and configured inward objective with room. Existing failed-breakout IDs retain their genuine excursion requirement. See [range interpretation](trends-and-ranges.md#wide-range-rotation-versus-tight-congestion-focused-synthesis).
