# Market cycle, pattern hierarchy and source fidelity

Skill guidance v0.10.0. Original synthesis of reviewed source passages, not a full reproduction or validated NIFTY strategy. Use alongside context adjudication, swing state and setup qualification. Preserve user scope and supplied operational policy.

## 1. Market phase precedes entry family

Assess initiative, trend/channel maturation, two-sided balance and credible transition. Record observable buying/selling pressure, follow-through, overlap, corrective depth, defended structure, accepted/rejected boundaries and remaining room. Neither a single bar nor a whole-day label establishes the current phase.

| Phase hypothesis | Evidence to assess | Primary participation question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initiating breakout/spike | Meaningful challenged structure, displacement, closes, follow-through, limited opposing success | Is an enabled initiating route still prospective, or is the first controlled correction developing? |
| Tight directional/small-pullback channel | Persistent progress, limited pullbacks, urgency, failed countertrend efforts | Is the apparent opposite signal just a flag? Has late extension/opposition changed the entry economics? |
| Broad channel/stairs | Directional swing progression with substantial overlap and two-sided corrections | Which full corrective structure survives a normal breakout test? Are objectives inward or continued swings? |
| Trading range/breakout mode | Shared-edge defense, two-sided acceptance, lost progress; compression may create breakout alternatives | Is it genuinely tight, or a wide range with supported inward edge entries? Has a real escape changed the context? |
| Transition/reversal hypothesis | Meaningful opposing strength, failed continuation, structure break/test and subsequent evidence | What has weakened, what has actually been defeated, and which enabled route fits the new initiative? |

These are hypotheses, not a mandatory linear state machine. A session can move between phases and contain a small range inside a larger trend. Breakout mode describes unresolved directional alternatives, not a forecast of the next candle or certain volatility. Always-in bias is qualitative evidence about pressure; it is not a requirement to hold a position.

## 2. Signal appearance depends on context

Separate prospective setup bar, actual trigger crossing, hypothetical/authoritative fill, entry bar and subsequent follow-through. Contract signal-bar references before a fill are an explicit prospective system convention; they must not imply a historical trade took place.

Strong-trend continuations can have unimpressive, small, inside or opposite-colored setup bars. Conversely, a strong-looking reversal bar can be an ordinary correction. A beginner-style strong-bar filter is a legitimate separately registered variant, but it is not universal Brooks eligibility. Record opportunity exclusion by policy separately from context unsuitability.

Propose `signal_assessment` with completed/developing status, observed shape, context role, contradiction, configured qualification mode or null, and policy eligibility. Suggested research modes distinguish STANDARD_CONFIRMATION, STRONG_TREND_CONTINUATION and REVERSAL_CONFIRMATION; they do not supply new numeric thresholds or automatically permit weak-bar entries. Unknown mode/policy means WAIT for an affected plan. Stronger reversal confirmation cannot substitute for missing reversal context.

Do not require a strong signal, strong entry bar and strong follow-through all to be known at initial entry: the latter occur later. Record their actual cutoffs and use them only for subsequent thesis review. Completed-bar entry confirmation remains the user's chosen baseline; discretionary early/close/limit entries stay outside enabled execution scope.

Supporting reasons must explain context, location or sequence rather than count overlapping pattern aliases as independent evidence. The source discusses multiple reasons and also strong-trend/second-entry exceptions; this is not a universal two-checkbox admission score. A good bar plus a pattern label cannot override incompatible parent context.

## 3. Leg counts are hierarchical

For H1/H2 or L1/L2, identify the parent corrective move, first crossing/attempt, interruption and renewed opposing activity, then the next attempt. In a continuing countertrend channel, several small crossings may be parts of one complex first leg. Cite the tiny/meaningful trend-line break or other registered interruption evidence that distinguishes them. Preserve competing parent and nested counts when unclear.

Two colors or two crossed previous-bar extremes do not independently authorize a second entry. A cheaper second entry can indicate strengthening opposition; it is a warning to reassess context, not a deterministic veto. H2 in a bull trend and H2 near the bottom of a wide range have different theses and objectives. H2 in a steep bear trend is not automatically a reversal.

First pullback is relative to its type: first bar-level pullback, minor-line break, moving-average test, moving-average gap or major structure test. These can occur in different orders or recur after acceleration. Record `pullback_type` and parent evidence as annotations; do not reset episode identity or enable a disabled family by giving an old opportunity a new “first” label.

## 4. Lines, microchannels and broad channels

Trend lines and channel lines require causal anchor definitions and publication/availability. A line break increases evidence of two-sided trading; strength, subsequent test and resumption/reversal matter. Redrawing a line from later pivots cannot improve an earlier interpretation. Exact line geometry is an operational choice, not a mandatory drawing algorithm in the source.

A first opposing break of a strong with-trend microchannel often begins a pullback rather than a major reversal. A microchannel against a larger trend can itself be a flag. Its direction alone does not decide the trade. Microchannel labels are observations unless a compatible enabled route and full policy are present.

Broad-channel tests can cross back beyond a breakout price while the larger swing remains defensible. “Back inside the breakout” is not a universal invalidation rule. Evaluate full parent structure, opposing effort and accepted continuation failure. Shrinking extensions can indicate weakening momentum; a channel overshoot can be exhaustion, acceleration or unresolved. No automatic countertrend entry follows. Brooks discusses two-way broad-channel trading, but advanced countertrend execution is not enabled by this project's scope.

## 5. Pattern shape does not define thesis

| Pattern observation | Distinctions required |
| --- | --- |
| Double bottom/top | With-trend corrective flag versus range-edge rotation versus test in a possible major reversal |
| Three pushes/wedge | Corrective pushes in the parent trend versus terminal extension; nested legs need not produce a geometrically perfect wedge |
| Triangle / inside-inside / inside-outside-inside | Compression and breakout alternatives versus continuation/reversal context; no automatic directional trade |
| Outside/outside sequence | Expanding local balance or failed attempt, not guaranteed reversal |
| Final flag | Potential late continuation structure before failure; confirmed failure afterward. Do not name a flag final from future outcome |
| Moving-average gap / prolonged separation | Trend-strength/maturation and possible extreme test; not a standalone trend direction or exact time-based certainty |

Recognize these as evidence tags without multiplying entries. The twelve-ID research profile remains unchanged. Wedge, double-test, microchannel, final-flag, major-reversal and MA-specific IDs need their own operational definitions, switches and evaluation before actionable enablement. Shape annotations can support an existing eligible thesis; they cannot bypass a disabled primary family.

An optional causal moving average can support Brooks interpretation if explicitly configured. Define length, price field, session continuity, warm-up and timestamps before use. Do not introduce a new indicator requirement, an arbitrary “20 bars” admission threshold, spot-volume/VWAP logic or volume/OI/options dependency. Numerical book examples remain teaching examples.

## 6. Breakout, failure and failed failure

Assess breakout strength relative to recent bars, structure crossed, follow-through and opposing success; distinguish initiation from a terminal large bar. A breakout's retracement is a developing failure/reversal attempt, not proof that the parent has reversed. Resumption can convert that attempt into a breakout pullback. If reversal gains credible acceptance, preserve the failed original thesis and its separate successor.

Describe this sequence causally: boundary known → excursion/escape → observed opposition → test/rejection/acceptance → resumption or meaningful reversal. Do not retrospectively relabel an unfilled/missed/consumed plan. A failed failure can be a new opportunity only under an explicit distinct-structure/reentry rule.

Tests may occur immediately or much later; neither an exact retest price nor a fixed number of candles is universally required. Missing a test defeats a breakout-pullback claim, not necessarily an independently complete initiating-breakout route.

## 7. Reversal burden and exhaustion

Separate a minor pullback/reversal, a tradable opposite leg, and a major trend reversal. For a major-reversal hypothesis assess the pre-existing trend, meaningful countertrend structural challenge, test of the old extreme and renewed opposing movement/acceptance. An early reversal entry can precede a confirmed major-trend classification; do not falsely mark the final state known or force every strong initiating breakout into an already-complete MTR template. Its enabled family still needs its own confirmation policy.

Climactic extension, consecutive unusually large bars, channel overshoot and failed progress can warn of corrective/range development. They do not guarantee an immediate reversal or authorize a blind fade. Contrast initiating strength with late exhaustion using position in the swing, prior duration/extension, obstacles and actual opposing effort. Participant trapping/profit-taking explanations are hypotheses inferred from price, not observed order flow.

A major reversal is not identified solely by a moving-average cross, line break, double top/bottom or opposite bar. The publisher's [chapter summary](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/9781119202622.ch3) distinguishes initial signals from a developed reversal. [Brooks's participant discussion](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/ask-al/strong-and-weak-bulls-and-bears/) also illustrates why a large late bar may attract profit taking rather than straightforward continuation.

## 8. Opening, session sequence and higher context

Assess prior close separately from prior intraday last price, prior session range/structure, current open and observed early attempt. Gap up/down can lead to continuation or reversal; neither gap filling nor trend-from-open is assumed. Prior trend strength can matter across sessions, but subsequent continuation is not guaranteed.

Keep hypotheses for opening trend, failed opening attempt, early balance, spike-and-channel, trending ranges, reversal and late resumption. A quiet midday range does not erase the morning initiative; watch its escape while respecting tight-range entry prohibition. A late resumption hypothesis must not override registered entry cutoff or force a new trade with insufficient remaining room.

Use exchange-local calendar and elapsed session position for NSE adaptation. US clock-time, contract/tick and frequency examples do not transfer numerically. Event/time effects require an explicit calendar/data policy; do not invent upcoming events or imply price-action inference knows their direction.

Five-minute decisions remain primary; completed higher-timeframe bars can clarify nested legs or daily location. Do not mandate a higher-timeframe confirmation for every entry, constantly change the analysis timeframe, or use developing/future higher bars as confirmed evidence. Retention and retrieval support context; they do not justify sending every historical candle/book at each call.

## 9. Movement, stops and trading mathematics

Measured moves describe conditional travel. Leg equality, spike extension, range height and measuring gaps use different anchors/conventions; magnets/confluence inform location but are not independent probability votes. Target-touch reaction is not certain reversal. Targets should remain supported by the selected move and opposition assessment, not chosen as the furthest attractive number.

Initial structural risk is known at plan creation; realized adverse excursion or later clarified actual risk is not known then. Risk-multiple objectives and structural objectives are both discussed by Brooks, but an adopted objective must be declared and justified before entry. Unknown success probability remains null; 40/60/80 teachings and model confidence do not establish an index-specific trader's equation.

Check feasibility: protecting a complete base of height H while targeting one height beyond its breakout gives risk roughly H plus entry displacement and remaining reward roughly H minus displacement. It generally cannot satisfy 2R. Do not “fix” it by using a tiny pause stop, doubling a target without premise, or silently dropping the configured gate. Wait for a better context-qualified entry, use a separately registered supported variant, or skip.

## 10. Consistent participation and management

Assess eligible initiating, first-pullback, second-entry and test routes instead of demanding only H2. Record why a supported swing could not be entered: policy exclusion, incomplete sequence, opposition, poor economics, latency, expiry, consumption or missing evidence. These are different opportunity-coverage results, not reasons to impose a trade quota.

Normal corrections and weak contrary signals do not automatically close an intact swing; meaningful disappointment and defeated objective/structure justify review under the declared exit policy. Do not automatically reverse after closing. Scaling, partials, trailing, limit entries and extreme scalping are distinct policy choices; understanding the books does not activate them. A planned add requires unchanged invalidation, available reserved risk and finite pre-entry terms. “Always-in” interpretation does not override flat/no-trade or risk limits.

Source locators and review limits are recorded in the three-book coverage register and project traceability matrix. Review of text adjacent to a figure is not visual chart review or a masked causal evaluation.

## Focused reversal review: claim, route and objective

Read when opposing evidence could change the current swing or a supplied position. Keep one current interpretation with its strongest contradiction, rather than maintaining every named reversal pattern as an independent thesis.

| Available evidence | Claim warranted | Participation implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One opposing bar or first break during an intact strong trend | Correction or reversal attempt | Preserve parent context unless further evidence defeats it; no automatic fade/exit |
| Meaningful opposing displacement with acceptance, or a qualified test/resumption | Supported new-direction swing hypothesis | Assess its actual enabled breakout/test/pullback route without requiring the completed day's major-reversal label |
| Prior trend, material opposing structural challenge, test of old extreme and renewed opposing progress | Developing major-reversal interpretation | Cite each event available now; early trade eligibility still depends on its registered route/policy |
| Sufficient sustained opposite trending behavior after that sequence | Major reversal supported at this later cutoff | Update interpretation now; do not backfill certainty into the earlier entry |

A prior trend is necessary to call something its reversal. A range-edge inward trade is not automatically a major reversal. Daily background and local swing can disagree: a supported intraday opposite swing can exist within an unchanged daily trend. Do not force alignment or declare a new local trend from one countertrend bar. Strong initiating opposition can establish an eligible new initiative without a textbook fully developed MTR, but only if the enabled route's actual structure and confirmation are present. Early countertrend MTR/wedge entries remain observations where no compatible registered entry route exists; mentioning source eligibility is not permission to activate a new family.

Three pushes, a double test, head-and-shoulders-like behavior or an expanding pattern can describe overlapping evidence. Choose the mechanism that matters: continuation correction, range-edge rejection or opposing test after a material challenge. Exact visual symmetry is not required by the teaching; the project's causal attempt/anchor definitions still must be supplied. Do not select convenient pushes retrospectively or add an engine for every shape. A wedge pullback in a supported parent trend differs from a wedge trying to reverse that trend. The latter needs stronger contextual/opposing evidence under policy. Source suggestions to wait for a second countertrend signal are not a universal two-signal rule for every initiating breakout.

Failure is relative to the declared objective. A move that made a small profit but missed a committed swing objective did not achieve that objective. It can still have been a profitable executed trade under an authorized early exit. Preserve target achievement, realized net result and market-premise change separately. Conversely, a protective stop need not defeat the parent trend. Failure can support an opposite route, renewed same-direction effort or balance; never automatically reverse, relabel a consumed episode or erase its result. Participant-trapping explanations remain inferred, not measured positioning.

The early reversal often first meets opposing structure or the origin of the pattern; a farther measured move requires a supported continuation premise. Source sequences that discuss a first target and a later extension do not activate partial exits or permission to move a committed target. Select the single initial objective under policy before entry and distinguish other conditional scenarios. Confirmation can reduce uncertainty while consuming reward. If the planned opposite swing cannot clear the first major barrier with viable economics, keep it observational rather than assign a distant target to justify it.

For supplied positions, meaningful opposing success or defeated defended structure warrants the registered premise-exit review. A developing pattern name or an isolated climax does not alone require closing. HOLD does not widen protection; CLOSE is a recommendation until authoritative execution confirms it. No automatic reversal, partials, averaging or new stop style is added.

Qualitative contrasts for review, not evaluated historical results:

- H2 below an intact steep bear trend: count alone does not justify a reversal long.
- Strong bull challenge followed by a weak bear test and supported bullish resumption: assess the actual enabled new-direction route; do not wait solely for a final major-reversal label.
- Three pushes down within a supported bull correction: assess continuation context rather than classify every wedge as countertrend.
- Double top near a range upper edge: assess an inward range thesis under its enabled variant; do not assume a whole-day bear trend.

The [publisher's MTR summary](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/9781119202622.ch3) likewise distinguishes early swing signals from developed major reversals. These passages do not calibrate NIFTY/BANKNIFTY probabilities or prove profitability.

## Session adaptation: opening, middle and close

Opening: retain supplied previous-session late structure and actual prior close, not just the gap sign. Assess early balance, initiative and failure using current evidence. Opening-range size relative to supplied completed daily ranges is context; it does not establish a trend day, guarantee range doubling or identify the final session extreme. Freeze the relevant range/projection anchors for each plan. Later expanded bounds cannot rewrite earlier decisions.

Middle: preserve morning initiative as background while judging current balance. Do not force continuation or forecast a late trap. Tight balance remains no-new-entry; an actual wide range can support its declared inward routes. Watch the real escape in either direction and assess its acceptance/test without fabricated follow-through.

Close: evaluate current structure and remaining permitted time. Explicit entry and holding/close cutoffs govern; no exact US source hour is imported. A supported setup may be excluded by session policy rather than poor context. Do not narrow stops, assume overnight permission or extend a pending expiry to create a late trade. Positions retain original geometry and declared close policy.

It contrasts sustained continuation, repeated early reversals and later opposing success. The completed charts were visible; this is source interpretation, not independent causal replay. A source's doubling of reversal-stop order quantity is not stop-price widening; neither behavior is enabled here.
